## Welcome

I am a 3rd year PhD student in Economics at the University of California, Davis. 

My interests lie in furthering econometric techniques in scenarios where shocks are spatially correlated, change is slow or there are repeat exposure. Specifically, I study migration responses to immigration shocks or climate change. 
In my research I combine structural models of the economy with causal-inference methods. Some of my applications use historical census data from the United States.
